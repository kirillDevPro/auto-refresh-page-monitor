Privacy-first by design

This extension only reloads your tabs. It requests no access to the content of the websites you visit, and your rules and statistics stay on this device.

  • No "read your data on all websites" permission
  • Your data is never sent to us
  • No external servers, no tracking, no telemetry
i Automatically resume active timers when the browser is restarted.
i When you are actively using the tab you are viewing (typing or moving the mouse), skip that tab's scheduled reload and try again at the next interval, so an auto-refresh never interrupts what you are doing. Uses the browser's idle state and applies only to the tab in focus - tabs in the background keep refreshing on schedule.
i Reload pages with cache disabled (equivalent to Ctrl+Shift+R). When off, normal reload uses cached resources where allowed.
i Automatically reload a monitored tab whose page failed to load (no connection, DNS error, timeout) or got stuck loading. It keeps retrying on a growing schedule (after about 30s, then 1, 2, 5, 10 and 30 min) and then every 30 minutes until the page loads or you turn it off. Requires a one-time permission and works only on monitored tabs. Per-tab and per-rule overrides are available.
i Sound notifications set the default for new timers in the popup.
i Vary each refresh by a random +/- percentage so reloads do not fire on a perfectly fixed schedule (helps avoid anti-bot detection on queues, restock and auction pages). Per-tab and per-rule overrides are available.
